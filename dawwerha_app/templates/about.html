About us

Plastic, metal, cardboard and copper pile up in our homes and streets. They have value, but most people don't know what can be recycled or who buys it.

Dawwerha connects people with local collectors and recycling centers, so less ends up in the trash and more goes back into use.

Our mission

Keep our streets clean by turning waste into income for families in the West Bank, and giving local collectors more work.

Why AI

Most people can't tell which materials are recyclable. A photo is easier than a guide.

Local collectors

We help scrap traders and recycling centers reach more people.
